1. Objective
Education loan support for students from Other Backward Classes (OBC), Economically Backward Classes (EBC) and De-notified Tribes (DNT).

2. Eligibility

2.1 Category
Applicant must belong to OBC, EBC or DNT category.

2.2 Annual family income
Annual family income limit for rural applicants is INR 1,00,000.
Annual family income limit for urban applicants is INR 1,20,000.

2.3 Age
Applicant age must be between 18 and 35 years.

2.4 Course
Undergraduate, postgraduate, professional, diploma and certificate courses are covered.

3. Loan amount and interest
Maximum loan amount: INR 12,00,000.
Interest rate: 10.5% per annum on the reducing balance.
Tenure: 12 to 72 months.

4. Repayment
Monthly installments on reducing balance. The repayment schedule is shared with the applicant before disbursement.
