1. Objective
This education loan supports Scheduled Caste students pursuing higher education in India by financing tuition fees, maintenance costs and related expenses.

2. Eligibility

2.1 Category
Applicant must belong to a Scheduled Caste (SC) family.

2.2 Annual family income
The annual family income limit is INR 81,000 for rural applicants.
The annual family income limit is INR 1,03,000 for urban applicants.
Applicants above the applicable limit are not eligible under this scheme.

2.3 Age
Applicant age must be between 18 and 35 years on the date of application.

2.4 Course
Course must be an undergraduate, postgraduate, professional, diploma or certificate course.

3. Loan amount and interest
Maximum loan amount: INR 15,00,000.
Interest rate: 10% per annum on the reducing balance.
Tenure: 12 to 72 months, monthly repayment.

4. Repayment
Repayment is monthly. EMI is calculated on a reducing balance basis at 10% per annum.
A moratorium may be available while the student is studying, as decided by the sponsoring institution.

5. Documents required
Category certificate, income certificate, admission proof, fee structure, bank account details.
