1. Objective
Small-ticket micro finance for micro-enterprises owned by target-group families. Priority is given to women-led enterprises.

2. Eligibility

2.1 Age
Applicant age must be between 18 and 60 years.

2.2 Annual family income
Annual family income limit is INR 1,20,000 for rural applicants.
Annual family income limit is INR 1,60,000 for urban applicants.

2.3 Enterprise vintage
The micro-enterprise must have been operating for at least 1 year.

3. Loan amount and interest
Maximum loan amount: INR 60,000.
Interest rate: 12% per annum.
Tenure options: 12 to 36 months.
Processing fee: 1% of the loan amount.

4. Repayment
Weekly or monthly installments, as agreed with the lending institution. EMI shown is indicative.
