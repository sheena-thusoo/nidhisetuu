1. Objective

Working-capital and equipment term loans for established micro-enterprises.

2. Eligibility

  • Applicant age must be between 21 and 65 years.
  • Annual family income limit is INR 2,00,000 for rural applicants.
  • Annual family income limit is INR 2,50,000 for urban applicants.
  • The enterprise must have been operating for at least 2 years.
  • Applicant must not have an existing loan in default.
